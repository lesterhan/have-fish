# have-fish 有鱼

年年有余 `nián nián yǒu yǔ` is a common blessing for having left overs each harvest. 有鱼 `yǒu yǔ` to "have fish" is often substituted as a wordplay, with iconographies of fish as representing prosperity each Spring Festival.

## What's going on

`have-fish` is a desktop personal finance app. Local first, privacy focused. No over-the-wire connections to your bank, but still super easy to use. Multi-currency at its core, so you can track your spending at home and abroad. Made with <3 in Canada.
