1The themes are from different design erasLight is 2003 Aqua Graphite. Dark is Nord — a 2016 editor theme with its own opinions. There was no dark Mac OS X in 2003, so dark has no period referent and borrowed one.
2The card is not a surface--card-bg is --color-window; the page is --color-window-raised. That is 1.06:1 light, 1.05:1 dark. Every panel in the app is separated from the page by a hairline and nothing else.
3In dark, losses are dimmer than gains#bf616a reads 3.05:1 — below the 4.5 floor §8 sets. #a3be8c reads 6.13:1. The two halves of the app's most important datum are not equally legible.
4Columns don't line up between groupsEvery SectionCard renders its own <table> at width:100% with no fixed layout, so each group sizes columns from its own content. Balance sits at a different x in Cash, Equity and Liabilities.
5Four equal tiles, four unequal factsAvailable is the number you came for; Investments is the one you can't act on and the largest, so it wins the eye. P6 asks for exactly one loudest element. There are four, then none.
6The inbox count is buried"8 need attention" is the thesis of the whole product and it is a chip in a filter bar, below the tiles, three rungs down the hierarchy.
7Red means two thingsA liability balance is red, and so is "stale 152d". One is a fact about money, the other a fault in the data. Owing $196 on an Amex is not an error state.
8The brightest pixel marks where you already areThe active sidebar item is the most saturated element on screen. Maximum contrast is spent on the one fact the user already knows.
9FLAGS is an empty columnThe header promises a place for flags; the actual flag renders under the date. Four repeats of the header row on one screen, one of them for a column that holds nothing.
10Selection owns the best real estateTwenty checkboxes sit in the leftmost column — the first thing the eye meets on every row — for an action taken maybe once a session.
11Dark has no structure to fall back onRules are 1.24:1 against the card. With the band invisible and the card invisible, dark has neither surface nor line to carry grouping. It reads flat because it is.
12The trust line is set as fine print"Ledger complete through Apr 23" is the app's central promise, rendered at the smallest size and lowest contrast on the page, at the bottom edge.
