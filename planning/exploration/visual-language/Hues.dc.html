Warm graphite has the strongest period pedigree that isn't Apple — SGI, NeXT and Sun all shipped warm putty enclosures — and warm greys are rare in software today, so it reads as a deliberate choice rather than a default. It also sits with 有鱼 and the 🧧 rather than against them. Instrument grey is the most severe and the most "measuring device"; it risks reading sickly at the light end. Slate violet keeps the cool feel you have now while stepping clear of Graphite's exact hue — the safest change, and the least character gained.
