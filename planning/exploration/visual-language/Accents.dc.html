Six accents, one rung

The accent is the only rung a user can move, so it is the only one that can break the ladder from outside the design. Today it does: ochre reads 2.64:1 in light and 9.52:1 in dark — the same token, a 3.6× swing, and four of the six fail 4.5:1 in light. Regenerating each at a fixed lightness while keeping its hue holds all twelve inside 4.65–5.47 without changing what any accent is.

Accent keeps marking the active nav item, but it no longer owns the loudest pixel: the nav highlight is a tinted panel with an accent hairline, while full-strength accent is spent once per screen on the count of what needs you. The generated values become ACCENTS[k].light.hex / .dark.hex; chipBg, chipFg and the titlebar gradient derive from the same hue at fixed offsets rather than being hand-picked per accent, which is where today's spread came from. Twelve assertions in tokens.test.ts — one per accent per theme — keep it there.
